\begin{abstract}
EpiScope turns routine health, climate and logistics data into district-level early warnings, one to four week
forecasts, an allocation of a fixed national stock and a seasonal preparedness calendar. It is built as a Spark
and Delta Lake medallion lakehouse over a synthetic encounter feed of more than 25 million records that takes
its population, geography and weather from real public data. At full scale (34 million encounters) the pipeline
runs in about 25 minutes on a 16\,GB laptop. On 35 planted outbreaks, EARS C2 detects 33 with a median delay
of eight days and fewer than two false alerts per district-year; the negative-binomial forecasts beat the
seasonal-naive baseline with a relative weighted interval score of 0.84; and at half the money needed, the
allocation meets 68\% of the need against 34\% for a population-based split. Only aggregated, small-cell
suppressed data reaches the D3.js dashboard.
\end{abstract}
