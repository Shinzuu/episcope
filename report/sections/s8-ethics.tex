\section{Ethical, Societal and Sustainability Considerations}
No real patient data is used; every encounter is synthetic and labelled so. Direct identifiers stop at bronze:
silver and gold hold only the keyed hash and age bands, the key never enters Git, and every export hides counts
from 1 to 4 with complementary suppression so that a total cannot reveal a hidden cell~\cite{sweeney2002k}.
One residual risk is known and documented in the code: the padded HMAC key appears in Spark's query plan, so
Spark event logs of the silver job must be treated as secret and kept outside the repository.
